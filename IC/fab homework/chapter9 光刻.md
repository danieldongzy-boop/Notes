#### 2.Immersion technology: Immersion technology can improve resolution and pattern fidelity by injecting a liquid medium (such as water) into the air gap of the lithography system to match the refractive index between the photoresist and the silicon wafer surface, thereby reducing reflection and scattering between the photoresist and the silicon wafer surface.
