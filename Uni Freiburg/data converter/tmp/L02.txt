                 Differential Nonlinearity – DNL

    • In an ideal world, all ADC codes would have equal width; all DAC output increments
      would have same size.
    • DNL(k) is a vector that quantifies for each code k the deviation of this width from the
      “average” width (step size).

    • DNL(k) is a measure of uniformity, it does not depend on gain and offset errors
       scaling and shifting a transfer characteristic does not alter its uniformity and thus
      DNL(k).
